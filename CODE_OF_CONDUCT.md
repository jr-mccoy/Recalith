# Code of conduct

This project is a small, experimental one. The expectations are correspondingly plain.

**Be decent.** Treat everyone in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions with respect. Disagree about the work, not about the person. Assume competence and good faith until shown otherwise, and say so plainly when shown otherwise.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, slurs or demeaning remarks of any kind; publishing someone's private information; sexualized language or attention; sustained disruption; retaliation against someone who reported a problem.

**Where this applies:** every space this project runs (the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and releases) and anywhere you represent the project.

**Reporting.** Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub (a direct message to a maintainer, or the private security-advisory route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if nothing else is available). Reports are read by the maintainers, kept confidential as far as the platform allows, and answered within seven days.

**Consequences.** Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, comments and issues that break these expectations, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban a participant, in proportion to the behavior and its repetition. A maintainer involved in an incident does not decide it.
