# Phased plan: from `0.1.0.dev10` to a publishable preview

Date: 2026-11-06 · Source: [the audit report](2026-11-06-memory-system-audit.md) · Finding IDs refer to its §3 tables.

## How to read this plan

Six phases, in dependency order. Each item names the findings it closes, the files it touches, what "done" means, and the test that proves it. Effort is in focused engineering days for one coding agent with the owner reviewing; it includes writing the tests. Owner actions are listed separately at the end of each phase; they are the only steps that need the owner's terminal or decision, and they are kept to the minimum.

Rules carried through every phase, from AGENTS.md: no edits to `src/`, `tests/` or `evals/` while a suite or measurement runs; every fix lands with its test at the owning task boundary; every engine change bumps the dev version and is recorded in BUILD_PROGRESS and, where it changes a contract or policy, in a decision record; no gate is weakened to obtain a pass; the owner runs every guarded or credential-bearing command.

The phases are sized so that Phases 0 to 3 can finish in about three to four weeks of agent work; Phase 4 overlaps with them; Phase 5 is the owner's live runs and the publication decision.

## What this plan does not do

It does not redesign anything. It does not relax a gate, remove a test, or widen a deny list. It does not touch the full profile, capsules, transfers, Codex, MCP or the side-by-side upgrade. It does not promise that the preview helps an agent; Phase 5.1 makes that question answerable, and the full release answers it.
