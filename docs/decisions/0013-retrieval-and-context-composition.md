# Decision 0013: Retrieval, context packets, pre-serialized views and the hook compiler

Status: accepted (Task 11) · Date: 2026-10-19 · Host observed: Claude Code 2.1.301 fixtures (capture); no live route (B1)

## Context

Task 11 builds retrieval and bounded context composition (SYSTEM_SPEC §5.1.1, §6.3, §7.3–§7.4, §8.1–§8.3; DATA_CONTRACTS §2.3, §9–§11; ACCEPTANCE_TESTS A01, A18, A23–A25, A69 and the Task 11 parts of A26, A31, A41, A58, A62) on the Task 8 applicability, the Task 9 admission and the Task 10 advice gate (`revalidate.guidance`). The documents fix the rules: the retrieval order with exclusion before candidate limits; FTS5 when available and a deterministic token index otherwise; the §8.2 delivery table; the exact estimate `ceil(UTF-8 bytes / 3)` and the budgets (start 2,000, prompt 1,000, pre-action 400, restoration 2,000, one shared 400 substitution allowance); pre-serialized candidate and fallback views for a stdlib hook compiler that never writes SQLite, never waits for a lock and reads at most 32 fallback rows; a final read-only fence on the guidance, policy and tombstone epochs; bounded live dependency checks (16 files, 1 MiB, 50 ms) with PEP 440 left in the worker; stale projections fall back or degrade. They leave open where the index lives and who maintains it, what a view holds, how "relevant" is decided, how the hook and the service stay equivalent, what "action-specific match" means for a caution, which purposes get views, what `manual` is budgeted at, how an open intent question is rendered, and what the hook does without a caller task. Task 11 needed one answer for each. Nothing here weakens a rule; where a rule was ambiguous the stricter reading was taken.

## Decisions

1. **Projections are disposable and maintained by the worker (schema 7).** `projection_state` (lexical backend, the store revision the index covers, the epochs and store revision the views were built from) and `retrieval_docs` (one row per indexed live head with its status, workspace and task scope and lexical terms) are new; `lexical_index` (from migration 1) holds word terms and structural keys (`path:`, `family:`, `tool:`, `subject:`); when FTS5 is detected (`index.fts5_available`, never assumed) the virtual table `lexical_fts` (rowid = the `retrieval_docs` rowid) is created at runtime as the lexical candidate source, else the token index is. The worker's deterministic projection step (`retrieval.compile.refresh_projection`, every poll after the planner, no model, no budget) brings the index to the store revision in bounded write transactions (at most 500 heads, whole store revisions, oldest first) and republishes all views when the guidance, policy or tombstone epoch moved. Projection writes never advance the store revision or the guidance epoch; a record's own transaction never touches them, so the index can lag but never lead. Migration `0007.sql`; `SCHEMA_VERSION` 7; a schema-6 development store is refused, never migrated silently (decisions 0007–0012; Task 17).
2. **Readers cover index lag directly.** `search.gather` reads `indexed_store_revision`; heads written after it (at most 512, `index.LAG_LIMIT`) are matched from their live bodies and override their stale index rows; more lag is reported `lexical_index_stale`. Every candidate is re-read from its live head before it is decided, so an index row is only a pointer, never authority.
3. **Exclusion before caps.** Status and workspace/task scope are filtered on `retrieval_docs` hot columns, then full tri-state applicability runs on the loaded record; a `false` never occupies a slot; the cap (`CANDIDATE_LIMIT` 64) counts eligible claims only. The bound task, its two latest episodes and its three latest certified results are separate bounded groups. Retired statuses are never indexed and never decided.
4. **Direct relevance.** A claim is relevant through an exact action-family match, a dependency or scope path matching the action's paths, the bound task (its scope, or support events bound to it), a lexical match (`manual` purpose: `explicit_query`), or a claim-dependency neighbor of a selected claim (`dependency_match`); the bound task's own state, episodes and recent observations are relevant through task continuity; an open intent question at session start or restoration, or through a member's or its own match. Quiet prompts stay quiet: nothing else enters automatically (§8.2 "direct relevance").
5. **The advice gate in packets.** Claims are decided by Task 10's `revalidate.guidance` (scope and conditions, the failure-lesson dependency gate over the bounded live values, freshness, status) and a caution additionally needs an **action-specific match**: the query action's exact family equals the lesson's, or the claim has action conditions (`action_kind`, `tool`, `path`) that all evaluate `true`. Rows of §8.2: candidates and **weak** claims are tentative (`tentative_context`, labeled) and enter only for the bound task or an exact action; `needs_revalidation`/`unknown` freshness is context with its label and the pending recheck reasons; a disputed claim is one `conflict` item with both sides (support and contradiction summaries); expired or redacted support — the claim's field, or every support event's live payload state — is omitted; retired statuses never appear.
6. **Open intent questions are one conflict item** (decision 0012 item 14): its member declarations are folded into it and never repeated as separate context; Task 12b deduplicates it per session.
7. **Rendering and the exact estimate are shared stdlib code.** `fast.context.render_item`/`render_packet` produce the exact text a packet is emitted as; `estimated_tokens` is `ceil(UTF-8 bytes / 3)` of that text (named an estimate). Assembly is greedy in rank order (an item that does not fit is omitted and counted, never truncated). Substitution (`substitute_pre_action=True`, supplied by the delivery branch when pre-action injection is unavailable): only an ordinary prompt with an included caution; cautions draw from one shared 400 allowance, everything else from 1,000, total ≤ 1,400 and ≤ the caller's maximum; restoration stays 2,000. `manual` (explicit inspection) is budgeted at 2,000.
8. **Views.** Per registered workspace and for its four most recently active tasks, one view per purpose (`session_start`, `prompt`, `pre_action`, `compact_restore`; at most 48/48/24/48 claims) holds ranked candidates in the primitive form the service also builds (`search.claim_candidate` and friends): rendered text, scope, conditions, precomputed version results with the digest of the exact consulted value, dependencies with recorded fingerprints, claim-dependency values with the head revision they were read at, action family, bound tasks, terms and support events; plus up to 128 ranked fallback rows per workspace. A view is written with the epochs of the very snapshot it was rendered from (one write transaction), so it can never be newer-labeled than its content. Views carry no wall-clock time and no store-revision dependence a hook checks.
9. **The hook compiler** (`fast.cache.compile_packet`; `fast.context.hook_compile_context` is the DATA_CONTRACTS name). Read-only snapshots: `mode=ro` URI (never `immutable=1`), `PRAGMA query_only`, `busy_timeout = 0`, a write-denying authorizer, the snapshot established at once or `store_snapshot_unavailable`. Task: the query's, else the session's worker-established task (`working_sessions`), redirected through linked placeholders; none means workspace-only. A view is served only while its guidance, policy and tombstone epochs equal the live store's (store-revision bookkeeping keeps it valid); otherwise, with ≥ 60 ms left, at most 32 fallback rows each kept only if its claim head is still at the row's revision and status, some support event still holds available evidence, and no newer tombstone names it; a policy change has no fallback (`fallback_policy_changed`). Degradations: `view_stale`/`view_missing`, `deadline_fallback_skipped`, `deadline_dependency_unknown`, `dependency_check_bounded`, `deadline_delivery_skipped` (< 40 ms), `foreign_installation`, `packet_invalid`.
10. **Stdlib twins and their proof.** The hook evaluates scope and conditions with `fast.context` (a twin of `core.conditions`; a version range is the precomputed result for exactly the consulted value, else unknown — no second version parser), the advice gate with `fast.context.guidance` (a twin of `revalidate.guidance`) and relevance with the same `fast.context.relevance` the service uses. `test_hook_and_service_decisions_agree` compares the strict and stdlib decisions on every candidate over 160 queries (it found a twin defect during development: disputed claims skipped the gate). Wire validators for `ContextQuery`/`ContextPacket`/`ContextItem` are generated (`tools/generate_hook_wire.py`) with stdlib twins of the item and packet rules.
11. **The final fence.** Service: `compile_context` gathers in one snapshot and re-reads the epochs in a fresh one; a change recompiles once (`fence_recompiled`), a second change returns an empty packet (`fence_epoch_changed`). Hook: `fast.fence.check` opens a fresh snapshot and requires equal epochs and every served head unchanged and servable (`fence_epoch_changed`, `fence_record_changed`, `store_snapshot_unavailable`), returning `fenced_at`/`fence_ms`. A change after the fence may race output already started; it is recorded, never claimed retractable (Task 12b's receipts carry it).
12. **Bounded live dependency checks** (`fast.context.DependencyBudget`): 16 files, 1 MiB, 50 ms, capped by the shared deadline minus the 40 ms fence tail; safe relative paths only, `realpath` containment, `O_NOFOLLOW`, regular files; the same value as `admission.fingerprint_file` (bytes digest; the recorded absent digest for a missing file). Excess, unreadable, escaping or skipped reads are unknown and suppress caution; nothing is executed. Both compilers use it, so a dependency edited outside any captured tool suppresses the caution at delivery even before ingestion notices anything.
13. **Failpoints.** `index.after_refresh`, `views.before_commit` and `views.after_publish` form `failpoints.RETRIEVAL`, armed only programmatically.

## Consequences

Wired now: retrieval, packets, views and fallback rows, the worker's projection step, the hook compiler and its fence, bounded live dependency checks, labels for candidates, weak claims, pending revalidation and disputes, open questions as one conflict item, recent observations. Consumer-side hooks for later tasks: Task 12a exposes `retrieve`/`compile_context` through the public service and CLI; Task 12b adds the delivery branch of `handle_hook` (session reservation, emission, receipts, suppression keyed by `(record_id, revision, condition_digest, purpose_class)`, per-session question deduplication, the session overlay's invalidation of older comparisons and supplying `branch_ref` and the query environment), passes `substitute_pre_action` when pre-action injection is unavailable, and adds `hook_record_delivery`; Tasks 15/16 must purge `retrieval_docs`, `lexical_index`, `lexical_fts` and `context_views` rows with the records they render and advance the guidance epoch for evidence expiry (the compiler already omits expired support); Task 17 reports projection lag in health. Limits: `config` dependencies stay unknown; view ordering among equal scores follows record IDs; the 10,000-claim figures are synthetic-corpus measurements. No live recall quality is claimed: ACCEPTANCE_TESTS §5/§6 retrieval gates stay unavailable until B1 clears.
